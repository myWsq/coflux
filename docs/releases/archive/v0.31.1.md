### Fixes

- **protocol**: Stop explicitly naming libc::time_t in logline. Its deprecation on musl caused both v0.31.0 Linux release builds to fail under -D warnings.
- **macos**: Pass NativeDiffHighlighter flush state through inout parameters instead of capturing mutable actor-method locals, resolving Xcode 26.6 Swift 6 region-isolation errors about sending hunk/result risking data races.

## Upgrade

- **daemon**: The center automatically upgrades online devices; workers hot-swap after signature verification. No manual action is required.
- **cofluxd CLI**: Unchanged in this release (`0.14.0`).
